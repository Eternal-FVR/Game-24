GAME 24

How To Play

The 24 Game is an arithmetical puzzle where the objective is to manipulate four numbers so that the final result equals exactly 24. Players must use addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, squares and square roots, utilizing all four numbers exactly once. In this game, you can choose between 3 game modes, Easy, Medium, and Hard, based on your skill. There are also Wildcard modes, the Competitive Mode and Speed Mode. Within these modes, you can use the 3 buttons, Skip, Check, and Back as well as a click on the spacebar to see how the computer solves the puzzle. When Competitive mode is activated, by clicking it, you have to try to beat the amount of puzzles you can solve in 1 minute, essentially competing against yourself. In Speed Round, the Game will ask you in a popup different time settings from 15 secs, 30 secs and 45 secs. The Main object of the game is to finish many puzzles in little time. You can Also check your stats in the header, with the Streak and Score. Change your Name in the Settings bar!
